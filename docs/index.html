🧠 Strategy

How the Bot Works

🧱

Order Block Detection

Identifies institutional Order Blocks on M5 and M15 timeframes. The ob feature has an importance score of 1.0 — the single most powerful predictor.

🤖

XGBoost ML Model

Trained on 75+ engineered features including SMC signals, momentum, volatility, and time-based features. Outputs trade confidence score.

🎯

Multi-Signal Fusion

Combines 4 signal types: RBS, SBR, Reversal Sniper & AI_XGBoost. Only high-confidence setups (≥60%) are executed.

🕐

Session Timing

Optimized for London (08:00-16:00) and New York (13:00-17:00 WIB) sessions when XAU/USD liquidity and volatility are highest.

🛡️

Risk Management

Dynamic SL/TP based on ATR. Max 2% account risk per trade. Spread filter to avoid high-cost entries. Position sizing via lot control.

📊

Regime Detection

Market regime detector switches strategy between trending and ranging conditions, adapting to current market structure automatically.
